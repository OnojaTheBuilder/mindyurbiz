The Host

Evan
"The Biz"

A self-made entrepreneur who turned a New York City hustle and out-of-the-box thinking into a business empire, then built a mic around it.

Evan created a multi-million dollar company during the heart of the pandemic, when most businesses were tanking, and has generated over $50 million in revenue as a serial entrepreneur. His work spans e-commerce, digital marketing, real estate flipping and developing, manufacturing, inventing and patents, film, TV and music, and business consulting.

He's an inventor and patent holder of an Apple iPhone accessory, a leader in sanitizing and disinfecting manufacturing, and a multimedia host who's put A-list guests and Fortune 50 CEOs in front of the mic. He's also used his success to give back, supporting causes from cancer research to children's education.
